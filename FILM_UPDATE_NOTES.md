- Fullscreen uses the full video width with a compact transport. Follow players, bench setup, and review open one drawer at a time. Detailed controls remain under More.
- Sound starts enabled. Mute and volume controls remain available beside playback.
- Pause ice time records a whistle interval while film continues. ON/OFF changes still work during the interval. Resume restarts counting without splitting a shift. Totals, ratings that use tracked time, and line-combination time exclude those intervals. Coach-entered official time retains precedence.
- Follow players is a local preview. Manually attach each player in a clear paused frame and confirm their current on-ice state. Reliable local matches retain that identity; continuous saved-line crossings record shifts. Uncertain matches, overlap, seeks, and detected camera changes stop the session and require reconnection. It cannot guarantee identity through occlusion or recognize players returning from an unseen bench.
- Existing appearance examples and unlabeled crossing review remain separate. Tracking labels are temporary and must be reattached after reopening film. Saved shifts use existing storage, Undo, and audit paths.

No live season data, coach-entered player statistics, storage path, or installed app has been changed. Tracking has been checked with synthetic frames, not validated against a full game recording.
