# Arctic Foxes 4.1.8

- Schedule now opens as a month calendar with a next-game summary, readable start times, date selection, month navigation, and a game-list option. Add games directly to a selected date; existing schedule entries and game links are preserved.
- Film Trim & breaks lets you set review start/end points to skip footage before or after the game, and skip marked whistles and breaks. Original videos remain intact. Skipping is disabled during active shift tracking or analysis.
- Player-follow preview now uses a bundled local player detector and tighter identity checks. It still needs coach tagging and review, especially when players overlap or move far away; it is not reliable unattended tracking.
- Existing saved statistics and the stable data location are preserved.
